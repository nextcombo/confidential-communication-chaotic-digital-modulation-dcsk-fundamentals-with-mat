# [Confidential Communication] Chaotic Digital Modulation DCSK Fundamentals with MATLAB Appendix

The randomness and unpredictability exhibited by chaotic signals, as well as their advantages in resisting frequency-selective fading and narrow-band interference, make chaotic signals well suited to meet the needs of confidential communication. Chaotic digital keying technology is a hot research topic in chaotic secure communication, with the differential chaos shift keying (DCSK) system being the most classic solution within this technology. Thanks to the use of the transmission-reference (T-R) mode, which reduces the interference between reference signal and information signal, DCSK systems have good bit error performance. However, the T-R mode also brings disadvantages such as low transmission rate and poor energy efficiency to DCSK systems.
